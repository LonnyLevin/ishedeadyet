about

Is He Dead Yet? is a daily SMS notification service. Subscribers receive one brief status update text message each morning at 6am Eastern time.

The service is free. Subscribers opt in by entering their phone number and may cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message.

This is a legitimate, fully operational service. Messages are sent once per day via automated SMS. No spam. No sharing of your data.
