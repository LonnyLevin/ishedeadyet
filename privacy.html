what we collect

We collect your phone number and payment information when you subscribe. Payment is processed by Stripe — we do not store your credit card details. We collect your phone number solely to deliver the daily SMS service you have subscribed to.

how we use your information

Your phone number is used solely to send you one daily SMS message. We do not use it for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

data sharing

We do not sell or share mobile or personal data with third parties, affiliates, or partners for marketing or promotional purposes. We only share data with third parties when it is strictly necessary to deliver our service and only under binding agreements that ensure confidentiality. Under no circumstances will mobile data be shared or sold for advertising or promotional use.

sms messages

By subscribing, you consent to receive one automated SMS message per day from Is He Dead Yet? Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency is one (1) message per day. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for support.

data retention

We retain your phone number for as long as your subscription is active. Upon cancellation, your number is removed from our system.
